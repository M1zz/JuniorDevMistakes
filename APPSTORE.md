### 설명

Know the mistakes before you make them. 100 Dev Mistakes is a growth checklist that covers the 100 mistakes junior developers make most, so you can grow faster and stop repeating the same errors.

The mistakes are organized into 10 categories: coding habits, debugging and problem solving, version control and collaboration, design and architecture, testing and quality, learning and growth, communication, infrastructure and deployment, security and privacy, and performance and optimization. Each one is explained in three parts: what happens, what to do, and what you learn from it.

Check off the mistakes you have experienced, then answer the reflection questions for each one. With 400 questions in total, the reflection journal turns a quick read into real understanding. You can follow your progress by category and overall across all 100 mistakes, and bookmark anything you want to revisit.

Your data syncs through iCloud (CloudKit) across iPhone, iPad, and Mac, so you can pick up wherever you left off. The app collects no personal information, and there are no analytics and no ads.

Try the first 30 mistakes (3 categories) for free. Upgrade to PRO to unlock all 100. PRO is available as a monthly or yearly subscription, with the yearly plan saving up to 50% compared to monthly, or as a one-time lifetime purchase.

Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your Apple ID settings.

Don't wait to learn it the hard way. Know the mistakes early, learn from them, and grow faster as a developer.

### 프로모션 텍스트

提前了解初级开发者最常犯的 100 个错误，勾选清单、写复盘，同一个坑不踩第二次。前 30 个免费体验。

### 설명

提前知道坑在哪，成长就能快一步。踩坑100整理了初级开发者最常犯的 100 个错误，帮你少走弯路，不在同一个地方摔两次。

全部错误分为 10 个分类：代码编写习惯、调试与问题排查、版本管理与协作、设计与架构、测试与质量、学习与成长、沟通、基础设施与部署、安全与隐私、性能与优化。每个错误都用三个部分讲清楚：会发生什么、正确做法，以及从中学到什么。

踩过的坑就在清单里标记为“已踩坑”，再回答每个错误配套的复盘问题。400 个复盘问题组成的复盘日志，能让你从“看过”变成“真懂”。可以按分类和整体查看 100 个错误的完成进度，需要回头再看的内容加上书签即可。

数据通过 iCloud (CloudKit) 在 iPhone、iPad 和 Mac 之间同步，换设备也能接着学。App 不收集任何个人信息，没有分析统计，也没有广告。

免费版可先体验前 30 个错误（3 个分类）。升级 PRO 即可解锁全部 100 个错误。PRO 提供月度订阅、年度订阅（比月度最多省 50%）和一次付费的终身买断。

订阅会自动续订。如未在当前订阅期结束前至少 24 小时取消，将自动续订并扣费。你可以随时在 Apple ID 设置中管理或取消订阅。
